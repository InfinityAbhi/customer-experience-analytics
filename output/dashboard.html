Customer experience analytics

NPS and CSAT survey analysis

Reproducible SQL and Python analysis of a 1,000-response, CC0 sample survey. Filter by customer segment and region to inspect recommendation, satisfaction, and text themes.

Recommendation bands

Promoters 9-10, passives 7-8, detractors 0-6.

Friction in detractor comments

Rule-based themes from optional text; counts cover commenters only.

Customer segments

Descriptive NPS and top-two-box CSAT. Small cells should be interpreted cautiously.

Monthly trend

Submission-month view, not a longitudinal customer cohort.

DATA AND INTERPRETATION

What this analysis supports

The survey is generated sample data, not actual customers or a company's business outcome. The source creates comments from a small repeated phrase pool and correlates satisfaction with NPS. The dashboard validates the analysis workflow and surfaces illustrative priorities. It does not establish causality, production adoption, or a measured improvement after a change.

CSAT uses ratings 4-5 as satisfied. NPS is percentage of promoters minus percentage of detractors. Blank comments are excluded from theme counts. Review the source dataset and CC0 license, SQL, and analysis output.
